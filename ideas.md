# AgriLink design direction

## Three stylistic directions considered

### 1. The harvest field note
**Intro:** Sun-warmed editorial with farm notes, natural-paper surfaces, and intimate seasonal storytelling. **Probability:** 0.07.

### 2. The rural trade desk
**Intro:** A clean, information-rich supply interface built around live availability, price context, inventory, and precise farm-to-buyer logistics. **Probability:** 0.06.

### 3. Common Ground market *(selected)*
**Intro:** A generous, modern cooperative produce market that pairs confident commerce with tactile crate labels, honest farm provenance, and warm local colour. **Probability:** 0.04.

## Selected direction: Common Ground market

### Design movement
Contemporary cooperative market: less like a generic enterprise dashboard, more like a carefully art-directed, neighbourhood produce hall that is genuinely good at trade. Borrow warmth and specificity from agricultural co-ops and market invoices; use modern interface craft for filtering, order state, and traceability.

### Core principles
Make origin as easy to understand as price. Prefer tangible harvest, grower, region, and quantity details to generic claims. Put verified identity, actual stock, unit size, and next harvest within arm's reach. Respect farmers as skilled sellers, buyers as decision-makers, and mobile use on real produce days. Separate illustrative demo signals from substantiated marketplace activity.

### Colour philosophy
Warm oat paper (#F6F5EF) sets an unbleached-market backdrop. Deep leaf green (#173B2E) is AgriLink's dependable action and identity colour. Spring acid-lime (#DDF17A) makes new harvest / key marketplace cues feel alive. Tomato terracotta (#CF6245) anchors price, freshness, and decisive emphasis without alarm. Ink (#20312B), market-sage (#718571), and pale green-gray lines provide legible hierarchy. Use rich photography for product colour, not decorative UI gradients.

### Layout paradigm
Editorial, asymmetric desktop marketplace home: narrow trustworthy utility strip, quiet wordmark navigation, prominent vertical stack of headline/search/filters, a left-anchored original harvest visual, and a compact lower-season/trust strip. Products sit in well-spaced market cards with product imagery, a legible title and price hierarchy, distinct farm detail, harvest/stock cue, and one clear buying affordance. Farmer/buyer workspaces use the same typographic and colour language with information-dense panels rather than a disconnected admin template. At small viewports, flatten the hero, keep search primary, wrap filters, and make product and order actions reach-friendly.

### Signature elements
A hand-cut monoline sprout-and-row emblem drawn as a single crest from seedling, furrow, and open crate. A practical “farm → harvest → market” provenance path. Small crate-stamp-style freshness/origin chips, crisp unit-price labels, subtle serial-style location/harvest references, and a transparent “where your money goes” line. Use check marks only to indicate explicit verification states—not to imply government or food-safety certification.

### Interaction philosophy
Calm and immediately responsive. Search and filters should never hide current results; status changes should name exactly what changed. Product, cart/order, and farmer-listing forms have concise validation and visible recovery paths. Use progressive disclosure for provenance, per-item details and settings; keep orders and inventory scannable. Keyboard-visible focus, reduced motion, descriptive image alternatives and sufficient contrast are default.

### Animation
Only purposeful 140–220 ms opacity/position changes for popovers, filter selections and save feedback; no bouncing vegetables or permanent attention cues. Honour `prefers-reduced-motion` and keep core information visible in a still state. The layout must not depend on animation for meaning.

### Typography system
Use system-first sans-serif UI typography, with a restrained, confident Georgia/serif display face for the hero headline and occasional story statements. Large but compact display type with short line lengths; human-readable, highly legible UI body copy. Tabular/lining numerals and predictable comma/currency formatting for prices, sizes, counts and metrics. Avoid synthetic handwritten fonts and all-caps long-form text.

### Brand essence
Good food, fairer trade, closer to the field. AgriLink makes local exchange more visible while giving the people who grow food an honest place in the deal.

### Brand voice
Warm, plainspoken, specific, and respectful. “Picked near Nashik, packed at first light” earns more trust than “farm fresh quality.” Use active verbs, describe what the market knows, and disclose what it does not. Call sample data “demo” or “illustrative.”

### Wordmark and logo idea
Set “agri” in a compact bold leaf-green sans face and “link” in a warm terracotta serif face. The original square product icon is a full-bleed dark-leaf-green canvas with a centered flat lime-and-warm-ivory mark: one sprouting leaf rising from two clear furrow strokes, whose negative space also suggests an open produce crate. No embedded letters or tiny detailing. Use the identical emblem for the header and favicon, keeping wordmark text outside the icon.

### Signature brand colour
Deep leaf green (#173B2E).
